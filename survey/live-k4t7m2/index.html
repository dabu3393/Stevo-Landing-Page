CONSENSUS SO FAR

The big theme: keep it simple. Nearly every comment, across all camps, comes back to clean layouts and quick-glance info. Scoreboard voters like that it sits "in the middle" — not too cluttered, not too bare — with today's bets and a big Make-a-bet button front and center. Launchpad voters want a high-level home screen they can dive deeper from, and several called it the cleanest and easiest to follow.

The Dashboard is losing the room. It has a loyal minority who love the trend graph and having everything on one page, but it's the most-named least favorite by a wide margin — "too busy," "too much going on," "too much of a statistics look" — and after 16 responses, not one person has called it easiest to use.

One caution on the Scoreboard: a couple of people don't love that the money up/down amount is the very first thing on screen — they want that info available, just not leading. Worth considering a subtler placement or a tap-to-reveal.

Color scheme: the dark theme is safe — most either love it or don't care. A couple would like an optional light mode; nobody prefers light overall.

Most-requested features: golf support keeps coming up (skins games, score tracking with course, date and notes), followed by a visible betting record / running totals (cash balance, won-lost by month and year), and a search function. One respondent tracks friend IOUs in a phone note — exactly the Settle feature, suggesting it deserves more prominence.

Positioning note: at least one person initially thought the app was for sharing sportsbook bets rather than tracking bets between friends — the "friendly bets with your crew" message needs to hit harder.
